## Objective
Fix the port configuration so the service can reach the container

## Hint 1
🔍 Observation Hint:

Check what port the container is listening on:
`kubectl get pod web-server -n k8squest -o yaml | grep containerPort`

Then check what port the service is forwarding to:
`kubectl get service web-service -n k8squest -o yaml | grep targetPort`

Do they match?

## Hint 2
🧭 Direction Hint:

The container is listening on port 80, but the service is trying to forward traffic to port 8080.

The service has two port configurations:
- `port`: What clients connect to (external)
- `targetPort`: What port on the pod/container to forward to (internal)

The targetPort must match the containerPort!

## Hint 3
💡 Near-Solution Hint:

Steps to fix:
1. Delete resources: `kubectl delete -f broken.yaml`
2. Edit broken.yaml and change the service's targetPort from 8080 to 80
3. Apply: `kubectl apply -f broken.yaml`
4. Test (optional): `kubectl port-forward service/web-service 8080:80 -n k8squest`
   Then visit http://localhost:8080 in your browser
