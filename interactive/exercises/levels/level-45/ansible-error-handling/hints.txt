## Objective
Fix the error handling to use failed_when instead of ignore_errors for proper error control

## Hint 1
The playbook silently ignores all errors, even critical ones.

## Hint 2
Use 'failed_when' for precise control over what constitutes a failure, instead of ignoring all errors.

## Hint 3
Replace 'ignore_errors: true' with 'failed_when: false' for the health check, and 'failed_when: disk_usage.rc != 0' for the disk check.
