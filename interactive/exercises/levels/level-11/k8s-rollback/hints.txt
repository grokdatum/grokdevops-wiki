## Objective
Rollback the deployment to the previous working version

## Hint 1
🔍 Observation Hint:

Check the deployment status:
`kubectl get deployment web-app -n k8squest`

Look at the rollout status:
`kubectl rollout status deployment/web-app -n k8squest`

What do you notice about the READY column and the rollout status?

## Hint 2
🧭 Direction Hint:

The deployment is stuck because the new image doesn't exist. Check:

`kubectl describe deployment web-app -n k8squest`

Look at the Events section. You'll see ImagePullBackOff errors.

Check the rollout history:
`kubectl rollout history deployment/web-app -n k8squest`

You can rollback to the previous version!

## Hint 3
💡 Near-Solution Hint:

Rollback the deployment to the previous working version:

`kubectl rollout undo deployment/web-app -n k8squest`

This will:
1. Revert to the previous ReplicaSet
2. Scale down the broken pods
3. Scale up the working pods

Then verify:
`kubectl get deployment web-app -n k8squest`
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest`

All 3 pods should be Running!
