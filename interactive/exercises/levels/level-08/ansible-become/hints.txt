## Objective
Fix the playbook by adding 'become: true' for tasks that require root privileges

## Hint 1
The tasks install packages and manage services - these need elevated privileges.

## Hint 2
Ansible needs privilege escalation to run tasks as root - look for the 'become' keyword.

## Hint 3
Add 'become: true' at the play level (same indent as 'hosts:' and 'tasks:').
