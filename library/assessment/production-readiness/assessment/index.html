Production Readiness Assessment¶

Context: You are about to go on-call for the Meridian platform for the first time. The system is described in detail in architecture.md. Read it thoroughly before starting this assessment.

Instructions: Answer these 50 questions to assess your readiness. Score yourself honestly for each question using the scale below. Record your scores in the scoring guide.

Scoring:

Score Level Meaning
3 Strong Could answer confidently with specific commands and steps
2 Adequate Know the general approach, would need to look up specifics
1 Weak Have heard of this but could not walk through it
0 No idea Completely unfamiliar

After Completing the Assessment¶

  1. Record your scores in the scoring guide
  2. Calculate your total and identify your readiness level
  3. Review per-section thresholds to find specific gaps
  4. Follow the study plan in study_plans.md that matches your results
  5. Check model answers in answer_key.md for questions you scored 0-1 on
  6. Re-assess in 2-4 weeks after completing the study plan
