  IGMP Snooping Querier Address: 0.0.0.0

Vlan 20:

### Switch IGMP snooping groups (VLAN 10 - source VLAN) ###

10    239.1.1.1        igmp    v2       Gi0/1, Gi0/2

### Switch IGMP snooping groups (VLAN 20 - receiver VLAN) ###

Switch# show ip igmp snooping groups vlan 20
Vlan  Group            Type    Version  Port List
----  -----            ----    -------  ---------
20    239.1.1.1        igmp    v2       Gi0/10, Gi0/11

### Note: The switch sees IGMP joins from receivers on VLAN 20, but without
### PIM on the router, the router never sends IGMP queries and never builds
### a multicast forwarding entry to bridge traffic between VLANs.
